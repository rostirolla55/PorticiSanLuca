Arch 3 - Portico of San Luca

The system of porticos leading up to the Colle della Guardia was built thanks to extraordinary collective participation: noble families, trade guilds, and wealthy citizens or religious figures financed the construction of one or more arches, leaving a plaque to commemorate their contribution. Count Gioseffo Carpegna financed the construction of Arch 3 of the Portico of San Luca. As a testament to the work completed, a commemorative plaque was made.
